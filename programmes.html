Programmes

Programmes for every stage of childhood

Crèche

Ages: [e.g. 6 months – 2 years]

A loving, secure environment where our youngest children are cared for like family while they begin to discover God’s world.

  • Trained, caring attendants
  • Songs, stories and sensory play
  • Daily routines that build security
  • Regular updates to parents

Reception

Ages: [e.g. 2 – 3 years]

The first step into structured learning, where children build listening skills, routine and the confidence to join a classroom community.

  • Early listening and following instructions
  • Bible stories and prayer
  • Colours, shapes and early motor skills
  • Social skills and good manners

Step-by-Step 1

Ages: [e.g. 3 – 4 years]

Hands-on activities introduce letters, numbers, shapes and colours, building the early skills every child needs before formal reading begins.

  • Letter and number recognition
  • Shapes, colours and patterns
  • Fine motor and pre-writing practice
  • Scripture memory and character building

Step-by-Step 2

Ages: [e.g. 4 – 5 years]

Building on Step-by-Step 1, children move into more structured letter-sound and number work that prepares them for Reading Readiness.

  • Letter sounds and early phonics
  • Counting, number sense and simple maths
  • Listening comprehension
  • Independent task habits

Reading Readiness

Ages: [e.g. 5 – 6 years]

Develops the specific skills a child needs just before reading independently — sound-letter recognition, blending and listening comprehension.

  • Phonics and sound blending
  • Sight word recognition
  • Pre-writing and early composition
  • Daily Bible-based reading practice

Learning to Read

Ages: [e.g. 6 – 7 years]

Children take their first confident steps into independent reading, combining phonics with comprehension in preparation for Grade 1.

  • Guided independent reading
  • Comprehension and vocabulary building
  • Early writing and sentence structure
  • Confidence-building practice

Grade 1 to 12

Grades: [e.g. Grade 1 – Grade 12]

A strong, consistent academic foundation that grows into rigorous, purposeful education — preparing students for external examinations, university, career and Godly leadership.

  • Individualized, mastery-based learning (A.C.E. curriculum)
  • English, Mathematics, Sciences and more
  • Character and values training throughout
  • Sports, arts, leadership and service projects
