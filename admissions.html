How to Apply

Joining our family is simple

We walk with parents every step of the way so your child settles in with confidence.

1

Enquire & visit

Contact us and tour the school to meet our team and see learning in action.

2

Apply

Complete the application form and submit the required documents.

3

Meet & assess

A friendly conversation and placement assessment help us understand your child’s needs.

4

Enrol & begin

Confirm your place, complete registration and welcome your child to a new chapter.

Documents needed

  • Completed application form
  • Birth certificate (copy)
  • Recent passport photographs
  • Previous school report / transfer letter (if any)
  • Immunization record
