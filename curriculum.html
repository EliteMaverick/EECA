Curriculum

The A.C.E. Curriculum

What Is A.C.E.?

A proven, Biblically based way to learn

Eternal Excellence Christian Academy runs on the Accelerated Christian Education (A.C.E.) curriculum — a system used in thousands of Christian schools worldwide. Instead of one textbook moving an entire class at the same pace, each pupil works through a series of small booklets called PACEs (Packets of Accelerated Christian Education), one subject and one step at a time.

Every PACE blends academic learning with Scripture, character-building goals and real-world application, so faith and knowledge grow together rather than in separate hours of the day.

How It Works

A typical PACE, step by step

1. Goals & Scripture

Each PACE opens with clear learning goals and a verse or character trait the pupil will practise alongside the academic work.

2. Guided Learning

Pupils work independently through explanations, examples and exercises at their own pace, with supervisors close by to guide and encourage.

3. Checkups

Short checkups along the way confirm understanding before the pupil is allowed to move on — gaps are caught and corrected early.

4. Self Test & PACE Test

A self test lets the pupil check readiness, then a final PACE test confirms mastery before starting the next PACE.

Why This Approach Works

Built around every child, not the average child

Academic Excellence

A rigorous, internationally used curriculum that stretches every pupil toward the highest standard.

Bible-Based Learning

Scripture and character goals are woven into every subject, not taught as a separate add-on.

Individualised Instruction

Each pupil moves at their own best pace — neither held back nor rushed ahead of real understanding.

Mastery-Based Progress

No moving to the next PACE until the current one is truly mastered, so foundations stay solid.

Goal Setting & Ownership

Pupils set daily goals and track their own progress, building responsibility and self-motivation early.

Critical Thinking Skills

Structured, step-by-step reasoning builds pupils who can think clearly and work independently.

From creche to Grade 12, one consistent approach

The same individualised, mastery-based method grows with your child — from early learning in our creche and nursery, through primary school, and all the way to Grade 12 — so transitions feel familiar rather than disruptive.
