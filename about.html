About Us

Our story, vision and mission

Who We Are

We see a seed of greatness in every child

Eternal Excellence Christian Academy sees a seed of greatness in every child — a seed that can be nurtured to greatness. Hence, we provide Biblically based, individualized and holistic education. Striving for Excellence & Integrity, we raise a “Total Child” who is spiritually, physically, intellectually, in character, emotionally and socially balanced.

We understand that God wants our children to be the change agents this world needs and to be sources of joy to us and to generations yet unborn. We are committed to the highest standard of academic excellence and character development that hinge on God’s Word, strengthening the lives of students and leading to the transformation of society.

Our Vision

To raise Godly children who will serve God in integrity and righteousness — children who will pursue and achieve all-round excellence. To raise “Total Children” one at a time.

Our Mission

  • Provide Biblically based, individualized, holistic education
  • Be the best in academic pursuit — the best or nothing!
  • Develop Godly character — to be like Jesus or nothing!
  • Train children to be useful to God’s kingdom and humanity
  • Train children to be emotionally and mentally balanced
  • Be a standard of reformed education
The Total Child

Six areas of balanced growth

Spiritual

Knowing and walking with God.

Intellectual

Academic excellence and a love of learning.

Character

Integrity, honesty and Christ-like conduct.

Emotional

Balanced, resilient and mentally healthy.

Social

Kindness, teamwork and service to others.

Physical

Healthy bodies, sport and discipline.
